03 — Who's behind it

Hey, I'm Dali.

I'm a 21-year-old martial arts tricker and trampoliner with a love for gaming, creativity, and building communities across Discord and game servers.

Tricking 0 started as a way to bring that same movement — flips, combos, flow — into a game. Mystic Gens and MHFarm grew out of the same instinct: build a small world, invite people in, watch it become something.
